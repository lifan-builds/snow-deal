What the numbers mean

Percentage off comes first. We divide the price reduction by the retailer reference price. Larger dollar savings break ties. Commissions, reviews, and images do not change that order.

A reference is not price history. “Retailer reference” is the compare-at price supplied with that exact variant. We have not independently verified that it was a usual selling price. We do not claim an all-time low.

Stock is an observation. “Reported in stock” reflects the retailer feed at the shown time. An exact-size filter requires reported stock. A retailer can sell out before you reach checkout.

Incomplete refreshes do not renew old prices. We retain the last complete source snapshot after errors, but it leaves browsing after {{ freshness_hours }} hours. Empty complete feeds remove old offers. Different catalogs have separate snapshots.

This is a limited source catalog. Coverage starts with the retailers listed above. We do not claim to search the entire market. Shipping, taxes, coupons, and membership prices are not included.
