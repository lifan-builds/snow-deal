KNOW WHAT YOU’RE COMPARING

Good deals need good evidence.

FreshPowder brings ski and snowboard retailer listings together so you can compare prices before buying directly from a shop.

One model, one season

Our comparison catalog starts with a selected set of reviewed retailer titles and product links. We match the model and season, keeping women’s versions, different widths and equipment bundles separate. Conflicting or unclear listings stay outside the comparison. Retailer titles can still be wrong, so check the product details before buying.

Your size changes the deal

We show sizes the retailer reports. A listed price may apply only to certain sizes or colors. A length filter matches reported values, not every number between the shortest and longest ski. Missing sizes stay unknown. USD and CAD prices are compared separately, and shipping, duties and taxes are not included.

A discount is not a price history

A percentage off compares the current listing price with the retailer’s supplied list price. It is not proof that the product normally sells at that price. Our catalog’s default order favors listings with photos and larger list-price discounts.

Comparison pages show actual price observations beginning when an offer was reviewed for that model. We exclude older history from potentially reused product URLs. The chart is the lowest observed listing price each recorded day; sizes and the set of shops may change. Short or incomplete history cannot establish a 30-day low, an all-time low or a rare deal.

Freshness you can inspect

Checks are scheduled daily. Listings older than 72 hours are excluded from current offers. Retailers can change prices or stock between checks. Partial and failed checks are visible in the store report; a failed check does not make an old price fresh again.

Reviews and recommendations

Expert-review links lead to third-party publications. Their scores are attributed on the linked source; they are not FreshPowder customer ratings or evidence that we tested the gear ourselves. Price comparisons do not determine whether a product is the right fit for you.

Free browsing, optional signup

Browsing and comparing do not require an account. Rare-deal email alerts are coming soon. Signing up records your interest; it does not activate email delivery yet. See our privacy and removal information.
