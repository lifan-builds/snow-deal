A SMALL LIST, A CLEAR PURPOSE

Email signup & privacy

FreshPowder is free to browse. Email signup is optional and does not create an account.

What you’re signing up for

A notice when rare-deal alerts launch, followed by occasional exceptional ski and snowboard deals. The service is in preparation; we are not sending alert emails yet.

What we store

We store your email address, signup time, the consent wording version and a private removal-token hash in our application database hosted by Turso. The site is hosted by Vercel. We use the signup data for these alerts, not to sell your address or enroll you in unrelated lists. The old invite waitlist is separate and is not automatically subscribed.

Changing your mind

The signup confirmation provides a private removal link. Keep it if you want to remove your signup before email delivery launches. Future emails will include an unsubscribe link. We will confirm email ownership before activating deal delivery.

Browsing data

We collect basic browsing and retailer-click events to understand which gear is useful. Hosting providers may process request information to operate the service. Retailer links take you to independent websites with their own privacy policies.
