# Selected machine evidence

Public product pages captured November 5, 2026 identify M1115 as a portable pressure sandblaster and M1114 as a benchtop blasting cabinet. Each includes the blasting equipment; neither is a standalone replacement component. Both require an external compressed-air supply. Product-page USD price, reference, SKU, new condition and reported stock agree with the collection observations. Exact URLs, timestamps and content hashes are in selected-machine-pages.json. Delivery options remain dynamically loading in the captured HTML, so these captures alone do not establish a location-specific delivery promise or fee.

[Grizzly shipping charges](https://support.grizzly.com/hc/en-us/articles/31751655125399-How-are-shipping-charges-calculated), checked November 5, distinguishes product-page freight and special-shipping charges from qualifying standard shipments. Freight and added services cost extra; do not describe all expensive machines as free shipping. [Freight deliveries](https://support.grizzly.com/hc/en-us/articles/4404704581271-How-do-I-receive-freight-deliveries) describes curbside delivery and separately charged liftgate service. Actual location-specific shipping and checkout totals remain unverified.
