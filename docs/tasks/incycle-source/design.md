Incycle supplies explicit BICYCLES or TURBO & ELECTRIC BIKE tags, with MOUNTAIN, ROAD, GRAVEL or ACTIVE descendants. Require the exact www.incycle.com product origin and exact generic taxonomy before interpreting these source-specific tags. Never globally whitelist Sporting Goods. Preserve negative condition/shipping/accessory gates and run the existing frameset branch first. Explicit electric tags override mountain category; BICYCLES plus observed category tags yields complete bikes. Reject contradictory accessory titles even under positive bike tags; do not infer identity from mountain/electric alone.
