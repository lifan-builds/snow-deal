# Consistent cash-savings tie breaks

Keep exact percentage off as the primary default ranking. On equal percentages, group USD first and then other currency codes, and prefer greater cash savings within the same currency, consistently with Big Ring and Bench Drop and the documented competitive product contract. Apply that rule when selecting an eligible snow variant and when sorting product cards; size/budget/reference filters still select before ranking/counting/pagination. Reviews/images cannot displace greater savings on a percentage tie. Percentages remain primary across currencies; no exchange rate or nominal cross-currency money comparison is introduced. Stable image/identity tie fallbacks and explicit alternative sorts remain available.

Acceptance: two variants at 50% choose the larger saving unless filters exclude it; a slightly higher exact percentage always wins regardless of money/images; legacy and variant-backed cards use the same order; pagination/counts and selected URLs agree. Mixed USD/CAD results cannot compare nominal cash savings; explicit currency filters retain the same within-currency order. No historical price, freshness or database-schema rewrites.
