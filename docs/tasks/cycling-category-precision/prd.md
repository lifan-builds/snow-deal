# Keep cycling equipment categories accurate

Status: in progress under the standing instruction to improve data quality and publish comparable audited releases.

Two verified gaps: a Dakine bike glove with generic Bike taxonomy is admitted as a complete bicycle; explicit singular/plural frame taxonomies are incompletely recognized, so a Mondraker frame is labeled a complete bike and other legitimate frames are dropped. Reject apparel when it is the primary product even under a broad bike taxonomy. Recognize explicit bicycle-frame taxonomy consistently before complete-bike fallback. Preserve existing condition, accessory taxonomy, shipping and stock gates.

A complete bike with an explicitly included light/rack/glove must not become an accessory solely because of that suffix. Do not classify a bike as a frame merely because its title describes frame material. Keep raw merchant names, options, SKU purchase links, price/reference and stock intact. No brand-specific or individual-product exceptions; no parser transport, request-budget, ranking or history-policy changes.

Acceptance: regression cases fail before/pass after; raw Gravity and Pro Bike Supply feed replay explains every addition/removal/category change; full existing public population is checked for unintended changes; a fresh full cycling candidate and expanded audit cover standard sample plus every newly eligible/reclassified in-stock listing before release. Remove the glove from the live unrestricted feed/facets and show the identified Mondraker item as Framesets. Preserve prior-day history and unrelated catalog data.
