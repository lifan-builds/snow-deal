# Competitive benchmark and improvement loop

Compare the snow, cycling and workshop/power-tool sites with relevant competitors. Define exactly ten repeatable metrics and improve product/data/UI gaps. Maintain deepest-percentage-first ranking on expensive products.

Acceptance: dated competitor registry with primary evidence; fixed metric definitions and thresholds; evidence statuses separate observed, claimed, blocked and unknown; comparable local before/after measurements; working improvements across the three catalogs; executable regression/quality gates; honest backlog where production coverage, independent audits or time-series evidence are not available. No unsupported claim that every competitor is beaten.

US/USD new equipment is the primary cohort. International comparators inform capabilities but cannot inflate US inventory comparisons. Retailers are sourcing/distribution alternatives, not grouped with multi-retailer aggregators. No public deployment, external messages or scheduled automation activation is implied by local improvement work.
