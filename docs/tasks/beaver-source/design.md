# Change boundary

Beaver exposes a Shopify feed with available=true even when the storefront says Usually Ships in 5-7 Days or Call for availability. The reusable Shopify feed remains the price/taxonomy mechanism; a merchant-specific subclass resolves its supported exact product pages through the existing paced detail-fetch path. Scope stock and price inspection to product-info[id^=MainProduct-], avoiding recommendations and quick-view markup. Bind data-product-id, canonical product path, hidden purchase variant and JSON-LD offer; never infer stock from an enabled cart button alone.

Files: new common parser/fixtures/tests; exact-host subclass choice in catalog refresh; independent main-panel merchant audit in catalog audit; tools source config after admission. No broad Shopify behavior changes or new feed framework. Preserve shipping notices through existing VariantOffer availability_note. No cart actions, authentication, or delivery-price claims.

The candidate review also found two Festool abrasive packs categorized as Power Tools whose names mention the compatible sander. Exclude explicit abrasives-for and sanding-discs/belts/sheets-for titles before machine matching, with a regression preserving complete sander kits that include abrasives. No unrelated taxonomy expansion.
