About FreshPowder

A simpler way to compare ski and snowboard listings across shops.

FreshPowder is built by Lifan. Browsing is free and does not require an account. We focus on equipment first; clothing and accessories are optional.

What we do

We collect retailer listings, review matches for selected models, and show reported sizes, observation times and the price history we have collected. We do not sell the equipment, inspect each physical item, or guarantee the retailer’s final price or stock.

Read our methods and limitations or use the buying guides.

Corrections and missing models

Open a public issue on GitHub with the FreshPowder page, retailer link, model/year and the detail that looks wrong. You can also suggest a model you would like compared. GitHub requires an account to submit an issue. Do not include your email, order number, address or other private information in a public report.

Commercial relationships

Retailer links currently take you to the shops. We are exploring affiliate programs; a future commission relationship will be disclosed alongside recommendations. A merchant’s commission should not determine which price is best for you.

Email alerts

Alerts are in preparation. You can join the email-only list; we are not sending deal emails yet. Read the signup and privacy details.
